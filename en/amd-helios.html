AMD Helios rack

No public price · No public unit price on the official page; these usually sell as systems, cloud instances or on request, so dealer quotes are not presented as official.

Answers how much HBM and FP8/FP4 compute one rack holds, not a single card. The official 31 TB is the sum of 72 MI455X GPUs; 1.4 EF FP8 and 2.9 EF FP4 are rack-level figures too. Confirm whether the configuration is 72 MI455X or MI450X — their per-card memory differs.
It does not claim: a single-chip comparison; a guarantee that buying the rack means running at peak; power, cooling and facility constraints are separate.
