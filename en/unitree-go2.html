Unitree Go2 quadruped

No public price · No public unit price on the official page; these usually sell as systems, cloud instances or on request, so dealer quotes are not presented as official.

Answers the weight, payload, endurance and perception setup of a quadruped body, which is what decides the floor space and experiment type it suits. Three figures are easy to misread: 5 m/s is a laboratory figure; endurance ranges from 1 to 4 hours depending on variant and battery; and the ~3000 W working maximum is a transient peak that must not be used to size average power. Price is excluded.
