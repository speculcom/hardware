AMD Radeon RX 9060 XT 16GB

$349 · US starting price for the 16 GB model.

An entry RDNA 4 16 GB card: 32 CUs, 2,048 stream processors, 128-bit GDDR6, 320 GB/s, 32 MB Infinity Cache, 25.6 TFLOPS FP32, 103 TFLOPS FP16 matrix (205 sparse), 205 TFLOPS FP8 (410 sparse), 160 W board power, a 450 W PSU recommendation and PCIe 5.0 x16. Capacity is generous but bandwidth is low; it fits budget 1080p/1440p gaming and Linux/ROCm local inference. On Windows many stacks still use Vulkan/DirectML, so confirm RDNA 4 support in your target framework before buying.
