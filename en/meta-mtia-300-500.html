Meta MTIA 300–500 (roadmap)

No public price · No public unit price on the official page; these usually sell as systems, cloud instances or on request, so dealer quotes are not presented as official.

Answers which generation Meta's in-house silicon has reached and what each generation is for. The official roadmap is four generations in two years: MTIA 300 is already in production for ranking and recommendation training, Meta's first training chip, with built-in NICs and communication offloading; MTIA 400 / 450 / 500 cover all workloads but are primarily for GenAI inference in 2026–2027; 450 and 500 are explicitly optimized first for GenAI inference. The cadence is one generation every six months or less, with modular designs that drop into existing racks and a PyTorch / vLLM / Triton / OCP software stack.
It does not claim: this is a roadmap card, not a per-chip spec sheet; Meta has not disclosed HBM capacity, compute, power or price for 300/400/450/500, so those fields are left empty. The widely repeated "4.5x HBM bandwidth and 25x compute from 300 to 500" figures were not found on the official pages fetched here and are not written. MTIA is not rented out; it serves Meta's internal workloads.
