How to use this provider

TensorWave fits teams that want dedicated GPU capacity without building a full public-cloud account. Compare contract length, capacity guarantee, interconnect and egress before choosing a cluster.

What to check before renting

Start with the job shape: short experiments, steady inference and long training need different billing models. Then calculate the total cost — GPU hours plus storage, egress, cold starts, reruns and staff time. A low GPU-hour rate can lose to a higher rate with better availability and cheaper egress.

Ask for the exact GPU model, whole-machine or per-GPU billing, minimum billing unit, region, interconnect and capacity guarantee. If the workload is sensitive, verify compliance scope for the specific region and service, not the provider's general certificate list.
