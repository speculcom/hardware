Google TPU 8i (inference)

No public price · No public unit price on the official page; these usually sell as systems, cloud instances or on request, so dealer quotes are not presented as official.

Answers which chip handles inference in Google's eighth-generation TPU line, and why it suits low-latency agent workloads. TPU 8i raises per-chip HBM to 288 GB and on-chip SRAM to 384 MB (3x the previous generation), pairs 19.2 Tb/s ICI with the Boardfly topology and more than halves the maximum network diameter, while the CAE offloads collective operations; Google says on-chip latency falls by up to 5x. A pod holds 1,152 chips, 11.6 FP8 exaflops and 331.8 TB of HBM; Google claims 80% better performance per dollar.
It does not claim: the launch page gives no per-chip FP4 figure (third-party reporting says 10.1 PF and is not written here); 11.6 EFLOPS is an FP8 pod total, not a single chip; the 80% and 5x figures are Google's, not independent measurements; no public pricing.
