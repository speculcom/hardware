NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX 16GB

No public price · No public unit price on the official page; these usually sell as systems, cloud instances or on request, so dealer quotes are not presented as official.

Answers the compute, memory and power triangle of a mid-tier on-board module: roughly a third of the AGX Orin 64GB compute and a quarter of its memory, but a 25 W class envelope instead of 60 W. It does not answer thermal design: a SO-DIMM module conducts heat through the carrier board, so sustained clocks depend on enclosure and airflow.
