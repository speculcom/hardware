Intel GPU D270 (Crescent Island)

No public price · No public unit price on the official page; these usually sell as systems, cloud instances or on request, so dealer quotes are not presented as official.

Answers what Intel offers when a standard PCIe server must run large-memory inference without rack-level liquid cooling. D270 is Intel's current data-center inference GPU: Xe3P, 32 Xe cores / 256 XMX engines, 160 GB LPDDR5X at 1.5 TB/s, 350 W, PCIe 5.0 air-cooled, native FP4 through FP64; one card holds up to 300B parameters and eight cards more than 1T. Its differentiator is large memory plus low power in a standard form factor, not peak compute.
It does not claim: 480 GB is a partner configuration, not the default Intel-branded card; 1.3 PFLOPS is FP8, not FP4; the 300B / 1T+ figures are capacity statements, not a promise of ideal throughput; Intel's comparisons use pre-production silicon and are vendor tests, not independent measurements; no public price or general-availability date.
