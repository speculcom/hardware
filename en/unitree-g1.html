Unitree G1 humanoid

No public price · No public unit price on the official page; these usually sell as systems, cloud instances or on request, so dealer quotes are not presented as official.

Answers the size, weight, degrees of freedom and endurance tier of a humanoid body, which is what decides the kind of experiments and floor space it suits. Price is deliberately excluded: quotations are commercial information that changes, and must be confirmed with the vendor. Control stacks and training frameworks are out of scope here and live in the robotics entries of tools and learn.

Key specs

dimensions
1320 x 450 x 200 mm standing; 690 x 450 x 300 mm folded
weight
about 35 kg with battery
degrees of freedom
23 (G1); 23-43 (G1 EDU)
knee max torque
90 N·m (120 N·m on EDU)
arm payload
about 2 kg (3 kg on EDU)
battery
9000 mAh; about 2 h endurance; charger 54V 5A
sensors
depth camera + 3D LiDAR; 4-microphone array
compute
8-core CPU base; higher-power module optional on EDU
