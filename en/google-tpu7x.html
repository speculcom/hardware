Google TPU7x (Ironwood)

No public price · No public unit price on the official page; these usually sell as systems, cloud instances or on request, so dealer quotes are not presented as official.

Answers per-chip HBM, FP8/BF16 compute and Pod size for the seventh-generation TPU. 192GiB HBM3E, 7.38TB/s and 4614 TF FP8 are per chip; 9,216 chips per Pod is the Pod footprint. It is not a GPU and cannot be slotted into CUDA-ecosystem selection logic.
It does not claim: Pod scale is not per-card throughput; 4614 TF is FP8 peak; not every framework is supported (the docs say TensorFlow is not supported, JAX/PyTorch are); it is not a claim that TPUs are stronger than GPUs.
