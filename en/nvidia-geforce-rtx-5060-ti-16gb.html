NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 Ti 16GB

$429 · US starting price for the 16 GB model; the 8 GB model is cheaper.

A budget 16 GB Blackwell card: 128-bit GDDR7, 448 GB/s, 4,608 CUDA cores, 759 AI TOPS, 180 W board power, a 600 W PSU recommendation and a $429 starting price for the 16 GB version. Its value is capacity, not bandwidth: a 4-bit 14B keeps context headroom, a 4-bit 30B can load but generates slowly, and 12 GB cards at the same price run out sooner. Fits 1080p/1440p gaming and “fit the model first” local inference, not throughput or training.
