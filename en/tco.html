Buy a card or rent cloud? Run the three-year numbers

This calculator does not choose for you; it puts an upfront purchase and an hourly rental on the same bill. Defaults are a consumer flagship plus a host; replace them with your quotes. All amounts are USD, excluding tax, network and colocation.

How to read the numbers

  • “Buy” = card + host + power + ops over the years; residual value, idle time and tax shields are excluded.
  • “Rent” = cloud rate × hours/day × 365 × years; storage, egress and long-term discounts are excluded.
  • “Break-even” = hours per year at which buying costs the same as renting. Below it, rent; above it, buy — provided the card is not idle.
  • This bill excludes time: drivers, environments, queues and troubleshooting cost people. Price your hourly rate and add it to the buy side.
