NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070

$549 · US starting price.

A mid-range Blackwell card: 12 GB GDDR7 on a 192-bit bus, 672 GB/s, 6,144 CUDA cores, 988 AI TOPS, 250 W board power, a 650 W PSU recommendation and a $549 starting price. 12 GB handles 4-bit 8B–14B models, but a 4-bit 30B still runs out of memory; bandwidth is 25% lower than the 5070 Ti, so token generation is slower. Fits 1440p gaming and entry local inference, not long-context 30B or dual-GPU training.
