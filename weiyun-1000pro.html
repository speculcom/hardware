回答「企业要在本地跑 800B 级模型，2U 机箱够不够」。官方口径：选配 GPU 后最高 13 PFLOPS、1.28 TB 显存、推理功耗低于 1 kW、2U；单机最高 800B 全参数，多机可扩。注意 13 PFLOPS 是选配后的峰值，不是实测吞吐；800B 是容量口径，KV cache、并发和框架都会改变实际可跑规模；价格与交付未公开。Answers whether a 2U box can run an 800B-class model locally. Official figures: up to 13 PFLOPS with optional GPUs, 1.28 TB memory, under 1 kW inference power, 2U; up to 800B parameters on one machine, expandable across machines. Note that 13 PFLOPS is a configured peak, not measured throughput; 800B is a capacity figure, and KV cache, concurrency and framework change what actually runs. Price and delivery are not public.
